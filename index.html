MetLife/Life insurance claims

Life beneficiary claims experience.

Designed how beneficiaries file a life insurance claim, starting from the landing page they reach through their claim kit. It explains the process in three steps: find the claim-kit code, create an account, and file. For beneficiaries who need help, a telephonic path lets a call center rep capture the claim on the call and send a secure link so they can verify, review, upload documents, and e-sign.

02 / ApproachThree principles refined over a decade

How I approach
the work.

I partner with cross-functional teams to translate research into design decisions, validate ideas through rapid prototyping, and ship work that delivers measurable business outcomes.

Anchor every project to a measurable outcome.

Before design begins, I establish what success looks like for users and for the business. A clearly defined metric, whether that's conversion, completion time, or satisfaction, shapes the trade-offs the team makes through the rest of the project.

Success metrics · Research plans · Trade-off decisions

Drive alignment through artifacts.

When stakeholders stall, the issue is usually abstraction. Journey maps, service blueprints, and decision logs make trade-offs visible and decisions durable. A team that can point to the same artifact moves faster than one that can't.

Journey maps · Service blueprints · Decision logs

Design within compliance, not around it.

I've shipped inside HMDA, TRID, TILA, and a range of fiduciary frameworks. Regulation is a design constraint, no different from accessibility or screen size. The strongest solutions make those guardrails feel like guidance rather than friction.

HMDA · TRID · TILA · Fiduciary frameworks
