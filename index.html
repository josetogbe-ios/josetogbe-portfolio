How I approach the work.

I partner with cross-functional teams to turn research into design decisions, validate ideas through rapid prototyping, and ship work that moves real business outcomes.

Anchor every project to a measurable outcome.

Before design begins, I define what success looks like for users and for the business. A clear metric, whether conversion, completion time, or satisfaction, shapes every trade-off after that.

Success metricsResearch plans

Drive alignment through artifacts.

When stakeholders stall, the issue is usually abstraction. Journey maps, service blueprints, and decision logs make trade-offs visible. A team that can point to the same artifact moves faster.

Journey mapsService blueprints

Design within compliance, not around it.

I've shipped inside HMDA, TRID, TILA, and fiduciary frameworks. Regulation is a design constraint like accessibility or screen size. The best solutions make guardrails feel like guidance.
